The central question is whether spending a talent point changes what players do rather than merely increasing a number. All ratings are subjective design judgments and not class-strength rankings.

This is the position stated or embodied by current documentation; original discussion history is not inferred.

## Audit #34: explicit methodological distinctions

- **Starting question:** The atlas says Babuni.dev began with Protection Warrior and asked whether improved abilities gain new interactions rather than only bigger numbers.
- **Scope:** Nine original classes, 27 trees and 466 talent classifications in an October 2026 beta snapshot, as reported by the atlas. These are source claims, not fresh recount results.
- **Criterion:** A talent is interesting when its primary design changes available play or interaction, not merely because it is effective. The atlas warns that numerical talents can be exceptionally strong.
- **Ambiguity:** Situational numerical bonuses, resource mechanics and active buffs can be borderline; closely ranked trees may shift if a few such judgments change.
- **Redesign examples:** Alternative talent mechanics are illustrative speculation and must not be presented as implemented or balance-tested.

No new design criterion was approved in the audit. Original discussion-level approvals beyond what the text supports remain unverified.
