# WoW Forever Talent Design Atlas — Editorial and Information Design

**Author:** Babuni.dev  
**Status:** Documentation of a historical October 2026 beta design study, not a game UI or addon.

## Reader journey

The [atlas](../README.md) opens with a specific design question about Protection Warrior: does a talent point make an ability do something new, or primarily improve an existing number? It then defines the rating criteria, compares nine classes, ranks 27 trees, and provides every one of the 466 individual judgments in class-by-class sections. Tree commentary, concluding comparisons and redesign exercises follow. Anchor links and return-to-contents references support long-form reading.

## Rating language

The study uses visually distinguished **Interesting** and **Boring** classifications. Those terms describe *mechanical novelty* in the author's analytical framework, never class power, player quality or objective balance. Numerical upgrades can still be important, even exceptionally strong. Editorial summaries should repeat this distinction wherever rankings are shown in isolation.

## Interpretation and edge cases

The author treats active interactions, new tactical decisions, conditional relationships between abilities and distinct consequences as mechanically interesting. Simple damage, cost, stat and cooldown changes are usually placed in the numerical category. Edge cases exist, and small differences in subjective classification can reorder closely ranked trees. Preserve explanatory notes beside rankings to avoid false precision.

## Portfolio treatment

Feature a readable selection of the historical Protection Warrior question, a nine-class summary, the full 27-tree comparison and examples contrasting a flat improvement with a changed interaction. Link back to the complete atlas rather than copying all 466 records into a promotional summary. The companion PDF should be described as a publication export, pending parity verification against the Markdown edition.

Do not present proposed talent rewrites as actual WoW Forever abilities or current live patch information.
