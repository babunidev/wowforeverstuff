## Retrospective reconstruction — 2026-10-26

A large personal talent-design atlas for World of Warcraft: Forever's October 2026 beta, comparing mechanical interest rather than only numerical power. The central question is whether spending a talent point changes what players do rather than merely increasing a number. All ratings are subjective design judgments and not class-strength rankings. The available work is structured as follows: README.md is a long-form research report covering all nine original classes, 27 trees and 466 rated talents, with the accompanying PDF edition. The repository is a design-study archive, not a runnable addon.

The source records substantive choices but not necessarily a full sequence of debate and approvals. Babuni.dev is the original author; AI organized existing material into a development record. No implementation or content was altered beyond these documentation files.
