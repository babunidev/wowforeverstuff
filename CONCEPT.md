# wowforeverstuff — concept

**Author:** Babuni.dev

A large personal talent-design atlas for World of Warcraft: Forever's October 2026 beta, comparing mechanical interest rather than only numerical power.

## Defining principle

The central question is whether spending a talent point changes what players do rather than merely increasing a number. All ratings are subjective design judgments and not class-strength rankings.

Synthesized from README and committed content, not complete past discussions.

## How the question became an atlas

The source explicitly says that the investigation began with **Protection Warrior**: what does the player actually gain when placing a talent point? A talent that changes an interaction, action or choice can be mechanically interesting, while a powerful reduction in cost or increase in damage may remain numerically straightforward. The author then applied this subjective lens to the nine original classes, 27 trees and 466 catalogued talents, retaining detailed commentary and tree-level conclusions.

The illustrative redesign section explores alternatives; it does not assert they exist in-game, have been balance-tested or were accepted by developers. This distinction is part of the original author's thesis.
